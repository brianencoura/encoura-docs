# Typography

The Encoura design system uses a curated type scale and three distinct font families, all configured via Tailwind CSS.

---

## Font Families

Three font families are available across the system.

### Museo Sans — `font-sans`

<div className="p-6 bg-navbar-gray rounded-lg mt-4 mb-6">
  <div className="font-sans text-lg text-navy-blue">The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.</div>
  <div className="font-sans text-sm text-grey mt-1">museo-sans · font-sans</div>
</div>

```html copy
<p className="font-sans">The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.</p>
```
